Day 1: The Rubber Boy

I found a strange fruit on the ship and ate it. It tasted terrible, but now my body stretches like rubber! It is called the Gomu Gomu no Mi. The only bad part is that I can never swim again. That is okay though, because I have nakama to pull me out of the sea. Today I started my journey to find the One Piece.

Day 2: The Crew Sets Sail

Our crew is growing! We have a swordsman, a navigator, a sniper and a cook who is always fighting with the swordsman. Nami keeps our Log Pose safe and Sanji keeps the kitchen full. Sometimes I eat all the meat before dinner, and then everyone shouts at me. We are heading for the Grand Line, and nobody is going to stop us.
